🛠️ How to Configure Webhooks

1

Create a Webhook

In your Discord server (Channel Settings > Integrations > Webhooks) or WeCom group (Add Bot), copy the generated Webhook URL.

2

Paste URL in Settings

Open Settings (gear icon) in the web dashboard, paste your Webhook URL into the integration panel, and click Save.

3

Test & Customize Rules

Select your alert filters (Intraday Alerts, Macro Briefings, or Full Research), click "Test Send," and receive an instant confirmation card in your channel.
